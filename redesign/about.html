About

Independent, ad-free, and reader-funded — a 501(c)(3) civic information hub for the whole county.

Ensuring thoughtful processes result in a livable community for all.

Our mission is to preserve the heart of Telluride by advocating for sustainable growth and transparent decision-making that prioritizes the long-term well-being of our residents. We work to safeguard the unique character of our box canyon home while fostering an inclusive and vibrant future for generations to come.

Where we came from

Livable Telluride grew out of “Let the People Decide,” a grassroots effort built around Measure 300 in November 2025. After that chapter closed, the organization evolved to focus on the broader, ongoing work of keeping Telluride functional, welcoming, and worth living in for the people who call it home.

How we work

We gather local news, government meetings, agendas, housing data, community organizations, and events in one place — explained plainly and always linked back to the original source, so you can check our work. We take no advertising, and everything we publish is free.
