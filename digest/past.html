THU, OCT 15Mountain Village
Mountain Village Town Council — Oct 15, 2026
The Mountain Village Housing Authority passed two items on second reading: an ordinance amending municipal code sections 16.01 and 16.02 to reference the annual fee schedule, and a resolution adopting the 2027 Housing Authority fee schedule including Village Court Apartment rents. A $0 application fee for deed-restricted housing de-restriction was noted. Council approved the fourth supplement to the intergovernmental agreement for gondola project development, committing $812,000 as Mountain Village's share of the 2027 planning budget. Council also approved a memorandum of understanding with San Miguel County and the Ridge at Telluride HOA clarifying the Coonskin viewplane height interpretation.

WED, OCT 14San Miguel County
Board of County Commissioners — Oct 14, 2026
Commissioners approved a consent agenda item authorizing the chair's signature on an amendment request letter for a local planning capacity grant, following the county's recent adoption of an expedited housing review policy. The $45,000 grant would direct $30,000 toward architectural and engineering fees for potential workforce housing at the Deep Creek Road and Bridge property, with $15,000 for personnel costs. Emergency Manager Shannon Armstrong will now operate with greater autonomy as her own department head, reporting to commissioners through the county manager rather than under the sheriff's direct supervision. San Miguel County ranked first in the state for increasing veteran benefits last year.

TUE, OCT 13Town of Telluride
Town Council Budget — Oct 13, 2026
Council held a budget work session covering enterprise and special-revenue funds for the proposed 2027 budget, with adoption scheduled for November 17. For water and wastewater, staff proposed a 12% rate increase for water and 18% for wastewater — along with matching tap-fee increases. On tap fees, a majority supported raising them higher than the base rate increases — 15% for water and 21% for wastewater — to begin building fund balances ahead of significant capital needs, including a new regional wastewater treatment plant. On affordable housing rental rates, council reaffirmed a 0% adjustment to AMI rent tables for 2027, deferring any structural changes pending a third-party policy review expected in 2027.
