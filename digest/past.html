THU, OCT 22Town of Telluride
Planning & Zoning — Oct 22, 2026
The commission continued the Shandoka lot redevelopment preliminary PUD to its December 17, 2026 meeting, requesting additional information before acting. The project proposes a mixed-use development at 860 Black Bear Road — combining a parking structure, affordable housing (80% deed-restricted), child care, a food bank, and a transit hub — with dimensional variations for building height, site coverage, and floor area.

WED, OCT 21San Miguel County
Board of County Commissioners — Oct 21, 2026
The board adopted three new natural-resources sections of the Land Use Code, each by unanimous vote. A companion Resolution 2026-43 cleans up related references throughout Articles 3, 4, 5, and 6. The board directed staff to prepare an MOU with Telluride Pines HOA to co-sponsor applications for state and federal loan and grant funding to restore the HOA's safe water supply — no county financial contribution was committed. Public Health Director Grace Franklin briefed the board on flu and COVID vaccine uptake, behavioral health initiatives including a half-million-dollar RHTP regional award, and a forthcoming strategic-plan refresh.
