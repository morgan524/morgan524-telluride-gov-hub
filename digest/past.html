WED, NOV 4San Miguel County
Board of County Commissioners — Nov 4, 2026
The board approved the 2027 BOCC meeting schedule (resolution 2027-38), adding a Telluride evening listening session on June 30 and an "if needed" designation for the Thanksgiving-week meeting. The board approved a 10% increase to the 2027 vegetation control and management fee schedule (resolution 2026-44), keeping fees below full cost recovery to encourage participation in the shared program with Ouray County. Resolution 2026-45, establishing revised housing authority administrative fees — including lowering the exception fee to $220 — passed unanimously.
