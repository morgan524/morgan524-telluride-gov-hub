Livable Telluride · Weekly Update
The Week Ahead Outlook
October 12 - October 18, 2026
📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

Monday, the Telluride Open Space Commission Site Walk skips the conference room and heads outdoors — members and the public meet at Rebekah Hall on West Columbia at 4:00 PM before heading up Tomboy Road for a High Country site walk. No votes, just firsthand eyes on the land, which is often where the real thinking happens. If you want to see how open space decisions get made before they ever reach a formal agenda, this is the meeting to join. Also Monday, the Bike & Brewery Tour rolls out of Mountain Village following the Jurassic and Meadows trails down to Telluride Brewing Co., with a complimentary beer at the finish — a fine way to close out a weekday.

Tuesday is a busy one. The Telluride Special Town Council Budget holds a three-hour work session on the 2027 budget — enterprise funds, open space, affordable housing, and the remaining funds are all on the table, though no votes will be taken. This is the unglamorous but consequential work of figuring out what the town can actually sustain. Across the county line, the Ouray County Board of County Commissioners has a full day at the courthouse, covering a Special Use Permit amendment for a Colona restaurant, the county's opt-in to Proposition 123's next affordable housing commitment cycle, a new intergovernmental law enforcement agreement with the City of Ouray, and a veterans' medical transportation program. The Ouray Planning Commission takes up a proposed ordinance regulating natural medicine businesses in Ouray — still in discussion phase, with a draft ordinance and supporting memo from staff. The Norwood Water Commission Meeting has a genuinely packed agenda: the Cossey Complaint hearing, a possible extension request on that same matter, two executive sessions for legal advice, contract approval with Land Right, and a review of SGM's Draft Environmental Assessment for the Redundant Line Project. And on the lighter side Tuesday, Tyler Simmons plays live on North Cora Street in Ridgway.

Wednesday brings three governing bodies. The Ouray County Board of County Commissioners Work Session goes all day — no formal votes, but the Ouray County Home Trust delivers a quarterly housing report, the Colorado River District gives its annual water briefing, and Friends of Youth and Nature gets time on the agenda. The San Miguel County Board of County Commissioners Work Session meets out in Egnar, the far western end of the county — no agenda items posted yet, but it's worth watching if you follow county business from that side of things. The Ridgway Town Council Regular Meeting is followed by the Ridgway Workforce & Affordable Housing Committee, which takes up two Home Trust of Ouray County items: Housing Services Director Eva Henson's Q3 2026 report — including a rehabilitated rental unit back in service, the first deed-restriction resale at Riverfront Village, and a DOLA supplemental — makes this one of the more detailed housing check-ins of the week. The Norwood Board of Trustees Meeting also meets Wednesday, though the agenda hasn't been posted yet. On the lighter side Wednesday, Elf The Musical open auditions begin at Telluride Middle-High School — Telluride Theatre is welcoming actors of all ages and experience levels, with performances running December 17 through 24 at the Sheridan Opera House.

Thursday, the TRAA Board of Commissioners Meeting holds its regular session — agenda not yet posted. The Mountain Village Town Council Meeting has a full plate: second reading of an ordinance updating Mountain Village Housing Authority fee language in the Municipal Code, a paired resolution adopting the actual 2027 MVHA fee schedules including Village Court Apartments rents, and a fourth supplement to the intergovernmental cost-sharing agreement for the gondola project's 2027 planning and development budget. Also Thursday, Facing the Mourning, a free grief support series, meets at the Redvale Community Center at 6:00 PM — open to anyone navigating loss.

Friday, Live Music at Alloy Kitchen in Mountain Village is free, featuring local artists including Apres Nova — one of several nights a week the venue keeps this going through the season.

Saturday, the OCSAP Grand Opening marks the launch of the Ouray County Sexual Assault Program at 112 Village Square West, Suite 220 in Ridgway — a real milestone for the region.

Sunday, The Young Fables play live on North Cora Street in Ridgway, closing out the week on the music side.

True North Youth Program

How you can help: Volunteer at a trail-work day or event (their activities calendar lists openings), donate, or — if you’re a teen — fill out a waiver (English or Spanish) and show up.
