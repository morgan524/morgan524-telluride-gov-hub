The Week Ahead Outlook
October 5 - October 11, 2026
📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

Monday, the big one to flag is up in Ouray, where the Ouray County Board of County Commissioners Special Joint Work Session sits down with the Ouray City Council to work through a draft Intergovernmental Agreement that would hand full law enforcement for the City of Ouray to the County — effective January 1, 2027. No formal vote comes out of a work session, but the direction set Monday will shape whatever comes next, and it's worth paying attention. The Ouray City Council Regular Meeting follows on the same day, so Ouray folks may want to clear their calendars. The Telluride Open Space Commission also meets Monday. On the lighter side, the Bike & Brewery Tour rolls out of Mountain Village — Jurassic Trail to Meadows Trail to Telluride Brewing Co. — if you want to earn your complimentary beer.

Tuesday, the Ouray County Board of County Commissioners Special Work Session zeroes in on the 2027 budget, with the Sheriff's Office getting dedicated time — notable given Monday's law enforcement conversation. Meanwhile, Telluride Town Council meets with a morning work session on the Flock Safety automated license plate reader system, with Chief Marshal Josh Comte presenting — a conversation about privacy and public safety that tends to get real wherever it lands. The formal agenda includes a public hearing on extending vesting rights at 221 W Colorado before that Certificate of Appropriateness expires October 18. CORAL SKYE plays live in Ridgway Tuesday evening if you need a place to land afterward.

Wednesday brings a full slate: the Telluride Housing Authority Subcommittee, the San Miguel County Board of County Commissioners Meeting, the Mountain Village Town Council Meeting, and the Telluride Ecology Commission all meet, with agendas still to be posted — check closer to the date. Over in Ouray, the Wright Opera House hosts A Monster Calls: Movie Night @ the Wright, doors at 6:30, film at 7:00 — a 2016 PG-13 film about grief and imagination that lands an 86 on Rotten Tomatoes.

Thursday, the San Miguel County Planning Commission Meeting runs most of the day, opening with a joint work session with the BOCC on an Affordable Housing Code Update — a sign the county is still trying to write policy fast enough to keep pace with a housing situation that hasn't eased. The SMART Board of Directors also meets Thursday; agenda pending. At the Sheridan Opera House, Not-So-Young People's Theater: Shrek The Musical takes the stage Thursday evening for anyone who wants something warmer than a budget discussion.

Friday, FLANNEL FEEDBACK plays live at the same Ridgway spot on Cora Street. Saturday, the Ridgway Star Party heads out to Frontier Field at 7:30 p.m. — the Black Canyon Astronomical Society brings professional telescopes and laser-guided constellation tours, free and open to the public, weather permitting. Sunday, Try Pickleball for Teens runs 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. at the Ridgway Pickleball Courts — registration required, contact Jazzmin at Voyager Youth if you have a teenager looking for something to do.
