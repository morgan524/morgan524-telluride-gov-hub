The Week Ahead Outlook
November 2 - November 8, 2026
📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

Monday, the most substantive land-use item of the week comes from Telluride /Special Meeting - Planning & Zoning Commission Chair, which is taking up a lot consolidation in the Historic Residential zone district — worth keeping an eye on even if it sounds routine, since how the town handles consolidations in that zone shapes what can be built there going forward. Also Monday, Ouray City Council Regular Meeting holds its regular session, though the agenda had not been posted at press time, and Norwood Water Commission Work Session meets with no agenda posted.

Wednesday, San Miguel County Board of County Commissioners Meeting meets, though no agenda has been posted yet, and Telluride Board of Education Work Session and Telluride Ecology Commission are also on the calendar without posted agendas.

Thursday, Mountain Village Design Review Board holds a meeting, though nothing of broad public consequence is listed on its agenda. Also Thursday, Telluride Special Meeting - Planning & Zoning Commission convenes for a special session with no agenda posted at this time.

→ Public Meetings This Week
MON, NOV 2 · 9:00 AMTown of Telluride
Town of Telluride /Special Meeting - Planning & Zoning Commission Chair
This rescheduled P&Z Chair meeting — moved from October 22 to November 2 — has a single item on it: a minor subdivision at 510 W Columbia Avenue/129 N Townsend Street. The application asks to vacate the internal lot line between Lots 39 and 40 in the West Telluride Addition and merge them into one approximately 5,625-square-foot lot. It's a lot consolidation in the Historic Residential zone district, not a new development proposal.

The Pinhead Institute

A Smithsonian affiliate in a box canyon — Pinhead has been getting local kids elbow-deep in real science for years, and its free Punk Science demos are a fixture in both Telluride and Ridgway.

How you can help: Bring the kids to a free Punk Science event, enroll them in an after-school class, donate, or mentor or host a Pintern.
