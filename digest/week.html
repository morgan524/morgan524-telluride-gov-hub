October 26 - November 1, 2026
📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

Monday, the San Miguel County Open Space Commission holds its regular meeting — worth a note if open land questions are on your radar.

Tuesday is a busier day: Telluride Town Council meets, as do both the Ouray County Board of County Commissioners and the Ouray County Board of County Commissioners Work Session — three public meetings worth checking for agendas if you have business with either body.

Wednesday, the main event is the San Miguel County Board of County Commissioners Work Session, where the county's governing board takes up land use, budget, and countywide policy — the kind of session where direction gets set before formal votes, so it's worth paying attention. The Ouray County Board of County Commissioners Work Session also meets Wednesday.

Telluride Adaptive Sports Program

Getting down a ski run or up a climbing wall looks different for everyone — TASP has spent nearly three decades making sure a disability doesn’t decide who gets to be out on this mountain.

How you can help: Volunteer (their site walks you through training and the interest form), donate, or pass their name along to a family who could use them.
