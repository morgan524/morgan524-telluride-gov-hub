📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

The biggest thing on the calendar this week is Tuesday, when both the Telluride Housing Authority and Town Council take up the same question: whether to convert two town-constructed units at 215 East Colorado Avenue from affordable ownership units with a price cap into employee dwelling units without one — a structural change to how those homes live in the housing stock, with public hearings on second reading also scheduled for Spruce House Unit H. Planning and Zoning holds a work session Wednesday on an early-stage employee housing proposal at the high school site, brought by the school district under an intergovernmental agreement with the town, while San Miguel County hosts a regional 5x5 meeting that includes a cross-jurisdictional conversation about a Southern Ute gas pipeline spill.

Telluride Housing Authority
Three substantive items at Rebekah Hall. First: a resolution to amend deed restrictions on two town-constructed units at 215 East Colorado Avenue — FINO 1A and 2A — converting them from affordable ownership units to Employee Dwelling Units and removing the maximum sale price, a structural change that rewrites how those units function in the housing stock.

TUE, SEP 22Town of Telluride
Town of Telluride Town Council
The most consequential action is a deed-restriction amendment for two units at 215 East Colorado Avenue (FINO II Units 1A and 2A) that would convert them from designated affordable units with a maximum sale price to employee dwelling units — effectively removing the price cap. That conversion question sets the table for three public hearings on second reading: the sale of Spruce House Unit H at 226 East Pacific Avenue, and both FINO II units 1A and 2A.

5 x 5 County Meeting - San Miguel County Hosts
This is an informal gathering — no votes, no decisions. The substantive item is a conversation with the Southern Ute Indian Council about a gas enterprise pipeline spill — the kind of cross-jurisdictional environmental matter that rarely surfaces in a single county's regular agenda.
