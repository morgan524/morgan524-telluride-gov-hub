📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

The Town Council heads into a retreat Thursday, and while the agenda isn't posted yet, those sessions tend to set the tone for everything from budget priorities to local policy — worth keeping an eye on. The Design Review Board also meets that morning with three multifamily projects on the table, including new units at Pennington Place and a continued review on Lost Creek Lane. On the social side, Telluride's monthly Art Walk runs Thursday evening from 5 to 7, and the Ridgway Rendezvous brings a free full day of live music to Hartwell Park on Sunday.

Design Review Board
Three multifamily projects are on the board's plate Thursday morning. First up is an initial architecture and site review for four new multifamily units at 100 Pennington Place (Lot 726-R1). Then the board returns to a multifamily building at TBD Lost Creek Lane (Lot 27A) — continued from June — for another initial architecture review. A conditional-use permit for office space at 620 Mountain Village Blvd, Unit 1A goes to the DRB for a recommendation to Town Council.
