Livable Telluride · Weekly Update
The Week Ahead Outlook
October 5 - October 11, 2026
📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

Monday, the Ouray County Board of County Commissioners Special Joint Work Session with the City of Ouray will discuss an agreement that would transfer law enforcement responsibility for the city to the county starting January 1, 2027. The Telluride Open Space Commission meets Monday afternoon at Rebekah Hall to get an update on the Valley Floor Signage Project's first phase.

Tuesday, the Telluride Town Council has a morning work session on the Flock Safety automated license plate reader camera system. Also Tuesday, the Ouray County Board of County Commissioners Special Work Session takes up the 2027 budget.

Wednesday is the busiest day of the week. The Mountain Village Town Council holds its all-day annual budget work session. The San Miguel County Board of County Commissioners also meets Wednesday with two public hearings: one on waiving land use application fees for Mountain Village's affordable housing project in the Ilium Valley. The Telluride Housing Authority Subcommittee meets Wednesday to consider a mortgage exception for a buyer under contract on a unit at 1001 E.

Thursday, the San Miguel County Planning Commission convenes for most of the day, opening with a joint work session with the San Miguel County Board of County Commissioners on an Affordable Housing Code Update. Also Thursday, the SMART Board of Directors meets, though that agenda hasn't been posted yet. On the lighter side, Shrek The Musical from Not-So-Young People's Theater opens Thursday at the Sheridan Opera House and runs through Sunday — a mixed-age cast of local performers bringing Shrek, Fiona, Donkey, Lord Farquaad, and the whole fairytale to life on that historic stage.

MON, OCT 5 · 4:00 PMTown of Telluride
Town of Telluride Open Space Commission
The Open Space Commission meets October 5 at 4:00 PM in hybrid format at Rebekah Hall. Three substantive items are on the table: a discussion with the Town Manager about the 2027 municipal budget as it relates to open space; an update on the Valley Floor Signage Project's first phase implementation and next steps; and a debrief following the Bear Creek Preserve Zone 1 Restoration Plan site walk.

MON, OCT 5 · 4:00 PMOuray County
Board of County Commissioners Special Joint Work Session with the City of Ouray
The Ouray County BOCC and Ouray City Council are sitting down together to work through a draft Intergovernmental Agreement that would have the County take over full law enforcement services for the City — starting January 1, 2027. The session runs through presentations, Q&A, public comment, and deliberations. A compensation schedule is attached as Exhibit A. No formal action comes out of a work session, but the direction set here will shape whatever formal vote follows.

Town of Telluride Town Council

San Miguel County Board of County Commissioners Meeting
A full day for the BOCC. On the planning side, two public hearings: one on waiving land use application fees for the Town of Mountain Village's affordable housing project in the Ilium Valley — a question that signals how seriously the county is willing to subsidize the process, not just the construction — and a lot line adjustment for Lots 320A and 320B in the Lawson Hill PUD. WED, OCT 7 · 10:00 AM–5:00 PMMountain Village
Mountain Village Town Council Meeting
This all-day special meeting is Mountain Village's annual budget work session — a deep dive into the 2027 budget across every department before formal adoption later in the year. The morning covers general fund revenues, administration, and the capital plan. After lunch, the focus shifts to economic development, the Tourism Fund, the Telluride Conference Center, and planning and development services. Transportation and parking — including gondola and chondola operations — get their own block, as do community grants and parks and recreation.
