📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

The big one to watch this week is Thursday's Planning Commission meeting, where three Land Use Code amendments — covering forestry practices, oil and gas, and geothermal energy — are up for the Commission's recommendation before anything moves to the BOCC. Wednesday is also stacked, with the County Commissioners' work session, the Ridgway Town Council, and the Board of Trustees all meeting the same day. On the lighter side, The Meditations bring classic roots reggae to the Sheridan Opera House on Friday, and there's a free hour of chamber music on the deck at the Telluride Science and Innovation Center Thursday evening.

THU, SEP 10San Miguel County
San Miguel County Planning Commission Meeting
Three Land Use Code amendments are on the table for the Planning Commission's recommendation — covering forestry practices, oil and gas, and geothermal energy. All three are code-change items, meaning the Commission is being asked to weigh in before any revisions move forward to the BOCC.
ℹ Why This Matters
Forestry regulations affect how private landowners manage timber, which in turn affects wildfire risk, watershed health, and ecosystem integrity. In a mountain community surrounded by national forest, these rules have outsized importance. Read the full Deep Dive →

SMART Board of Directors
The regional transit authority board — routes, funding, and operations. The agenda hasn’t been posted yet — details will follow closer to the meeting.
ℹ Why This Matters
The gondola (built 1996) is critical regional infrastructure. The current funding agreement expires in 2027. Ballot Issue 3A approved ~$8.2M/year in new tax revenue, but a replacement gondola is estimated at $120-150M+ -- leaving a significant funding gap. Read the full Deep Dive →
