📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

The meeting to put on your radar this week is Town Council on Tuesday, which opens with a work session on automated license plate reader cameras — the Flock Safety system — with Chief Marshal Josh Comte presenting; wherever ALPR technology lands it tends to split a room, so that conversation is worth showing up for or at least following closely. Tuesday also brings a public hearing on extending the vesting period for property rights at 221 W Colorado, a project whose Certificate of Appropriateness expires October 18. Also on Wednesday, the Telluride Housing Authority Subcommittee, the Board of County Commissioners, and Mountain Village's Town Council all meet, with agendas still pending, and the SMART Board of Directors and Planning Commission convene on Thursday. Monday's Open Space Commission and Wednesday's Ecology Commission round out a genuinely full week of public business. On the lighter side, Not-So-Young People's Theater: Shrek The Musical hits the Sheridan Opera House on Thursday, A Monster Calls: Movie Night @ the Wright screens in Ouray on Wednesday, and Alysha Brilla brings her JUNO-nominated sound to The Sherbino in Ridgway on Saturday.
