📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

The biggest thing on the calendar this week is the San Miguel County Board of County Commissioners meeting on August 5th, where three Land Use Code amendments go to public hearing — including changes to the 'Qualified Owner' definition, a wildfire areas update, and a continued hearing on nonconforming lots, all moving toward formal resolutions. That same Wednesday, the Ouray County Planning Commission meets virtually to work through possible changes to the definitions section of their Land Use Code — the kind of quiet, foundational work that shapes what gets built and what doesn't. Thursday brings the Telluride Town Council retreat to set 2027 goals, and the Design Review Board takes up three multifamily projects in Mountain Village, while jazz and the Ridgway Rendezvous round out the week on a lighter note.

WED, AUG 5Ouray County
Meeting
Ouray County Planning Commission meets virtually August 5th, 2–4 PM, for a work session on possible changes to the Land Use Code's Section 2 — the definitions section. No in-person attendance. Work sessions like this are where the real shaping happens: definitions determine what the rules actually mean in practice, and a tweak to how a term is defined can quietly shift what gets permitted, what gets denied, and how future applications get read.
Meeting info →
THU, AUG 6Town of Telluride
Town Council Retreat
The August 6 retreat is a single three-hour work session in which Council will set the Town's goals and objectives for 2027. No votes, no land-use items — just the annual exercise of deciding what this Council wants to prioritize in the year ahead. Those priorities, once set, tend to shape budget decisions and staff direction for the whole cycle, so the conversation matters even if nothing is formally adopted.

THU, AUG 6Mountain Village
Design Review Board
Three multifamily projects are on the board's plate Thursday morning. First up is an initial architecture and site review for four new multifamily units at 100 Pennington Place (Lot 726-R1). Then the board returns to a multifamily building at TBD Lost Creek Lane (Lot 27A) — continued from June — for another initial architecture review. A conditional-use permit for office space at 620 Mountain Village Blvd, Unit 1A goes to the DRB for a recommendation to Town Council.
