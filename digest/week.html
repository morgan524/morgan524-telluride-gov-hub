The Week Ahead Outlook
November 9 - November 15, 2026
📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

Monday, the Telluride Intergovernmental Meeting gets the week started, joined by the Telluride Open Space Commission, which has three items on its agenda, and the San Miguel Basin Fair Board, which has no posted agenda.

Tuesday, over in Ouray County, the Ouray County Board of County Commissioners meets at 9 AM with two public hearings on its agenda, including a 2026 budget amendment and the Curry Regular PUD, a request to subdivide a 14.42-acre parcel on County Road 1 into two lots and open space; at 3 PM, the Ouray County Board of County Commissioners Work Session takes up back-to-back presentations from EcoAction Partners and San Miguel Power Association covering energy and environmental programming. Also Tuesday, the Ouray Planning Commission takes up two substantive matters, and the Norwood Water Commission Meeting convenes with no posted agenda.

Wednesday, the Ridgway Town Council and the Norwood Board of Trustees both meet with no posted agendas.

Thursday is the week's most consequential day. The San Miguel County Planning Commission holds a morning work session on proposed amendments to the Affordable Housing Impact Mitigation Fee — a structure that directly shapes whether and how workforce housing gets built across the county — along with a broader discussion of housing incentive ideas; after lunch, the commission takes up a county-initiated rezoning of a portion of Placerville Park near the fire station to bring its zoning in line with its actual public use. The SMART Board of Directors also meets Thursday with no posted agenda.

Town of Telluride Open Space Commission
The Open Space Commission takes up three items Monday. TUE, NOV 10 · 9:00 AMOuray County
Ouray County Board of County Commissioners
The November 10 BOCC meeting carries a full load. The afternoon brings two public hearings: the Curry Regular PUD, a request to split a 14.42-acre parcel on County Road 1 into two lots and open space via a combined preliminary/final development plan; and a 2026 budget amendment. In general business, the board takes up a request to repeal Resolution 2025-043 — the austerity resolution that has been governing hiring — alongside four separate vacancy-fill requests under that same resolution for EMS and Road & Bridge.

TUE, NOV 10 · 3:00 PMOuray County
Ouray County Board of County Commissioners Work Session
A work session, so no formal votes — but the direction set here shapes what comes next. The afternoon opens with back-to-back presentations from EcoAction Partners and San Miguel Power Association, covering an hour on energy and environmental programming.
