📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

The biggest thing on the calendar this week is Wednesday's HARC meeting, where three separate public hearings will take up the Carhenge redevelopment at 700 W Pacific Ave — multiple new buildings on Lots 34 and 34B, with the Town itself as applicant through Design Workshop, all in an Accommodations 2 zone outside the Historic District. The Board of County Commissioners also meets Wednesday, though their agenda hasn't been posted yet. On the lighter side, the week brings Library Game Night with free pizza at The Alibi Monday evening, Music on the Green with Sway Wild in Mountain Village Friday, and Heather & Douglas at The Courtyard in Ridgway Sunday.

HARC Meeting
The July 15 HARC meeting is dominated by the Carhenge redevelopment project at 700 W Pacific Ave — three separate Preliminary Large-Scale public hearings covering Buildings A, B, C, D1, D2, E1, E2, and E3 on Lots 34 and 34B of Backman Village, all new construction outside the Telluride Historic Landmark District in an Accommodations 2 zone, with Design Workshop as applicant and the Town itself as owner.
