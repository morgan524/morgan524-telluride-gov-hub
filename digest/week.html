September 28 - October 4, 2026
📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

The biggest thing on the calendar this week is Wednesday's Special Town Council session, devoted entirely to an appeal hearing on the Carhenge lot subdivision — specifically whether to uphold P&Z's July decision to consolidate Lots 34 and 34B at 700 W. Pacific Avenue into a single parcel, a move that has to happen before any redevelopment of that open-space site can proceed. Also on Wednesday, the Board of County Commissioners holds a work session and HARC convenes to review exterior changes in the historic district. Thursday brings a full slate: a Special Meeting - Planning & Zoning Commission, a Town Council Budget session, the Lodging Tax Panel Meeting, and the Design Review Board, where two multifamily projects are on the table — 15 employee apartments at 306 Adams Ranch Rd and four new units at 100 Pennington Pl. On the lighter side, Monday's Bike & Brewery Tour rolls from Mountain Village down to Telluride Brewing Co., Ken Gentry & the Companions play The Alibi on Thursday, and Saturday Ouray throws a free 150th anniversary concert at Fellin Park headlined by Big Head Todd & the Monsters.

Town of Telluride Special Town Council
This special session is entirely devoted to one thing: a Town Council appeal hearing on the Carhenge lot subdivision. On July 23, 2026, P&Z unanimously approved consolidating Lots 34 and 34B at 700 W. Pacific Avenue into a single parcel — a preliminary step the Town needs before any redevelopment can move forward on that open-space site.
ℹ Why This Matters
Town Council is considering a Planned Unit Development at the former Carhenge site — a potential deed-restricted workforce housing project near the gondola. This is a work session: no final vote, but the direction set here shapes the formal application. Key questions: how many units, at what income levels, and how will parking and neighborhood impacts be managed? Read the full Deep Dive →
