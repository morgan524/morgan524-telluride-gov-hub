The Week Ahead Outlook
November 30 - December 6, 2026
📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

Wednesday opens with the Telluride Housing Authority Subcommittee at 9:00 AM, taking up a deed-restriction dispute over a Colorado Avenue unit at Gold Run — the resale figure in question sits at 57% of the original $279,200 purchase price, well above the 30% absolute cap written into the older deed restriction. The Telluride Ecology Commission meets Wednesday afternoon at 4:00 PM; no agenda has been posted.

Thursday is a full day. The Telluride Town Council Retreat opens at 9:00 AM for a three-hour work session in which Finance staff will walk the Telluride Town Council through a comprehensive overview of Town funds — no votes, just the kind of detailed budget briefing that sets the table before the formal process moves forward. Also at 10:00 AM, the San Miguel County Board of County Commissioner meeting -Special takes up 2027 budget adjustments, including clarifying the wildfire mitigation budget, a $50,000 seed-money request from the Chamber of Commerce, and an additional cost tied to a Youth Hangout project. The Mountain Village Design Review Board also runs Thursday from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM. The Telluride Planning & Zoning Commission meets Thursday evening at 5:30 PM in a special session rescheduled from November 5; no agenda has been posted yet.

Friday, the Telluride Special Town Council meets at 10:00 AM to hear two related appeals over a building HARC approved without formally determining which design guidelines apply — specifically whether the structure qualifies as a rated Supporting reconstruction or as new construction on a vacant lot — and whether HARC's amendment of a sequencing condition from its 2021 demolition approval was made with the findings the Land Use Code requires.

→ Featured Organization
Second Chance Humane Society

Since 1994 the region’s stray and surrendered pets have had somewhere to land — and the thrift shops in Ridgway and Telluride quietly help fund the whole operation.

How you can help: Adopt or foster (applications on their site), volunteer at the shelter, donate, or drop off goods at either thrift shop.
