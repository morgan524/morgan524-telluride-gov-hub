📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

The biggest thing to track this week is Tuesday's HARC meeting, where a work session will examine a potential demolition and new construction of Rebekah Hall — 113 W Columbia, the Town-owned building where HARC itself meets — with HOK as applicant; that one deserves attention. Monday's Housing Code SSR also continues its eighth session refining the proposed Community Housing Zone, which is real, slow-moving work that could eventually reshape what gets built in unincorporated San Miguel County. Sprinkled through the week are the Fire District board, the BOCC, Ophir's General Assembly, and a Telluride P&Z special session on the Comprehensive Plan update, plus Kipori Woods playing a free patio show in Ouray on Tuesday and Stillhouse Junkies at the Wright Opera House on Wednesday.

→ Public Meetings This Week
TUE, SEP 15San Miguel County
San Miguel County Housing Code Update SSR
The Stakeholder Strategic Roundtable (SSR) working group convenes for its eighth session on the San Miguel County Housing Code Update — a Proposition 123-funded effort to strip out Land Use Code provisions that slow workforce and affordable housing production in unincorporated areas of the county. The two-hour working session zeroes in on the proposed Community Housing Zone, refining draft code recommendations that will eventually go to the BOCC and Planning Commission as formal redlines.

WED, SEP 16Town of Telluride
Town of Telluride HARC Meeting
Two items stand out on this HARC agenda. First, a work session on a potential demolition and new construction application for Rebekah Hall itself — 113 W Columbia, the very building where HARC meets — a Town-owned property in the Residential/Commercial zone, with HOK as applicant. That's worth watching. Second, the Phoenix Market at 221 W Colorado Ave comes in for a Certificate of Appropriateness extension along with a vested property rights extension, which goes to Town Council as a recommendation.
ℹ Why This Matters
A proposed 900-space parking garage at Shandoka would be one of the largest structures in the region. Combined with Chair 7 and the gondola redesign, these projects represent a massive escalation in commercial infrastructure. WED, SEP 16Ouray County
Ouray County Meeting
Ouray County's Planning Commission meets at the Ouray Courthouse for two public hearings. First up is the Curry Regular PUD — a formal planned unit development application that will get a full public hearing. Second is an exemption application from Clifford Pastor to subdivide his parcel into two lots. Both items require public hearings before the PC can make a recommendation, and packet materials are available through the county's agenda portal.

THU, SEP 17Town of Telluride
Town of Telluride Special Meeting - Planning & Zoning Commission
A special P&Z work session covering two tracks. First, the ongoing Comprehensive Plan update — consultants and staff will walk through the Existing Conditions StoryMap, review where Phase 2 community engagement stands, and present Survey #2. The Comp Plan process sets the long-range framework for land use, density, and character across town, so these check-ins matter.
ℹ Why This Matters
A Comprehensive Plan revision sets the rules governing all future land use and zoning — changes that can reshape development patterns for decades. This is one of the most consequential items any planning body considers. Public input at this stage carries the most weight; once formal review begins, options narrow quickly.
