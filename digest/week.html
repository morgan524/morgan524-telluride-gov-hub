The Week Ahead Outlook
October 12 - October 18, 2026
📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

Friday brings a full slate before the weekend: the Telluride Special Town Council Budget meeting is on the books — agenda not yet posted, so watch for details — and over in Ouray, both the Ouray County Board of County Commissioners and the Ouray Planning Commission hold their regular meetings. The Norwood Water Commission also meets Friday, another one without a posted agenda yet. On the lighter side, the Bike & Brewery Tour rolls out of Mountain Village Friday, following the Jurassic and Meadows trails down to Telluride Brewing Co. — a scenic way to close out the week with a complimentary pint. In Ridgway, Tyler Simmons plays live on North Cora Street.

Saturday, Elf The Musical auditions from Telluride Theatre open at Telluride Middle-High School — actors of all ages and experience levels are encouraged, and the show runs the Sheridan Opera House stage December 17 through 24.

Monday is a busy one for county governance: the Ouray County Board of County Commissioners holds a work session, and the San Miguel County Board of County Commissioners holds their own work session out in Egnar — the far western end of the county, worth watching for agenda updates if you follow that side of things. The Ridgway Town Council and the Norwood Board of Trustees both hold regular meetings Monday as well, neither with agendas posted yet.

Tuesday, the TRAA Board of Commissioners meets for their regular session, and the Mountain Village Town Council holds its regular council meeting — no agenda posted on either yet, but both worth keeping on your radar. Also Tuesday, Facing the Mourning, a free grief support series, meets at the Redvale Community Center. Friday, Nattali Rize brings her message-driven music to The Alibi in Telluride. And come Sunday, The Young Fables play the North Cora Street venue in Ridgway to close out the week.

How you can help: Volunteer at a trail-work day or event (their activities calendar lists openings), donate, or — if you’re a teen — fill out a waiver (English or Spanish) and show up.
