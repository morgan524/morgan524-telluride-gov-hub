📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

The meeting to watch this week is Monday's Housing Code Update SSR session — housing code work at the county level can carry real consequences for how and where people live here, even when the agenda posts thin, so it's worth keeping an eye on. Also Monday, the Town Council sits as the Liquor Licensing Authority to consider KOTO's permit for its Live at the Drive event on July 30th, and the Open Space Commission holds its regular meeting. Mid-week, a free public panel at the Sheridan Opera House takes on climate and energy with three outside experts, doors at 6 pm Tuesday, RSVP required.

WED, JUL 29Town of Telluride
Parks & Recreation Commission
A rescheduled midday meeting with a short agenda. The one action item is a request from the Telluride Humane Society to use Elks Park and the River Trail — Maple St. to Gold Run — for a dog-friendly 5K fun run on October 10, 2026. The event, called Tails on the Trail, expects around 100 attendees, runs 8:30 a.m. to noon, and would generate a maximum of $72.25 in admissions fees to the Town. Street use on Colorado Ave. is still pending staff review.
