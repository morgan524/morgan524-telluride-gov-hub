The Week Ahead Outlook
November 30 - December 6, 2026
📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

Tuesday, the Telluride Housing Authority Subcommittee meets at 9:00 AM — no agenda has been posted, so we don't know yet what's on the table.

Wednesday is a full day. It opens with the Telluride Town Council Retreat at 9:00 AM — a three-hour work session where Finance staff will walk the Telluride Town Council Retreat members through a comprehensive overview of Town funds. No votes, no action items; this is the numbers-on-the-table briefing that happens before the formal budget process moves forward, and it's worth knowing about. Also Wednesday morning, the San Miguel County One Time Event convenes at 9:30 AM with no posted agenda. The Mountain Village Design Review Board follows at 10:00 AM for what is scheduled as an extended session running into the afternoon. Then Wednesday evening, the Telluride Planning & Zoning Commission meets at 5:30 PM — no agenda has been posted.

Thursday, the Telluride Special Town Council meets at 10:00 AM to hear two appeals of a HARC decision. At issue is whether HARC ever formally determined which design guidelines apply to a building — specifically, whether it qualifies as a rated Supporting structure reconstruction or as new construction on a vacant lot. The Telluride Special Town Council will also take up whether HARC properly followed Land Use Code requirements when it amended a sequencing condition from a 2021 demolition approval.

Second Chance Humane Society

Since 1994 the region’s stray and surrendered pets have had somewhere to land — and the thrift shops in Ridgway and Telluride quietly help fund the whole operation.

How you can help: Adopt or foster (applications on their site), volunteer at the shelter, donate, or drop off goods at either thrift shop.
