WED, OCT 21 · 5:30 PMTown of Telluride
Town of Telluride HARC Meeting
The October 21 HARC meeting is anchored by the Shandoka Lot Redevelopment Project — the Town of Telluride's own large-scale development at 860 Black Bear Rd, zoned Accommodations 2. HARC takes up Buildings 1 and 2 as one public hearing item and Buildings 3 and 4 as a second, both continued from August 19. Each application covers new construction outside the Telluride Historic Landmark District exceeding 5,000 square feet of floor area under LUC 7-203.B.2.a.1.
ℹ Why This Matters
A proposed 900-space parking garage at Shandoka would be one of the largest structures in the region. Combined with Chair 7 and the gondola redesign, these projects represent a massive escalation in commercial infrastructure. More parking directly drives higher visitor volumes — permanently affecting the character of downtown Telluride. Read the full Deep Dive →

THU, OCT 22 · 10:00 AMSan Miguel County
San Miguel County Board of Adjustment Meeting
The San Miguel County Board of Adjustment meets October 22 with one substantive item: a variance request from a Mountain Village parcel (Parcel #456536200023) to reduce the required setback from County Road 63L right-of-way. Board members will visit the site — located across from the County Jail — at 9 a.m. before convening. Approval of October 2025 minutes is also on the agenda. The meeting opens with a moment of silence for Jim Botenhagen.

THU, OCT 22 · 5:30 PMTown of Telluride
Town of Telluride Planning & Zoning Commission
The October 22 P&Z meeting carries two items of broader public consequence. First, the Shandoka Lot Redevelopment — a Preliminary PUD application for Town-owned property at 860 Black Bear Rd — finally gets its hearing after being continued from the September 24 meeting due to staff capacity. The proposal would increase certain dimensional limitations in exchange for public benefits on the 4.07-acre Accommodations 2-zoned parcel.
