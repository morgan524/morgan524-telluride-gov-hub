The Week Ahead Outlook
October 19 - October 25, 2026
📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

On Monday, the Ouray City Council kicks off the week with one of its meatier agendas in a while — and fittingly so, since the city is marking its 150th anniversary at this very meeting. Meanwhile, the Norwood Planning and Zoning Commission works on a Land Use Code Update and a Master Plan Update.

Tuesday, the Telluride Fire Protection District Board of Directors opens its 2027 budget process with a first reading, and the Telluride Board of Education meets that evening for an update on mill levy override funding. The Ophir General Assembly also meets, though its agenda hasn't been posted yet.

Wednesday, the San Miguel County Board of County Commissioners takes up four Land Use Code amendments that would add a new Natural Resources article to the county code, with separate public hearings on forestry practices, oil and gas operations, and deep geothermal operations. Telluride HARC also meets, but its two Shandoka hearings have been pushed again, without discussion, to November 18. The Rico Board of Trustees holds its regular meeting, agenda pending.

Thursday at 5:30 PM, the Telluride Planning & Zoning Commission takes up the Shandoka Lot Preliminary PUD, where the Town, as owner and applicant, seeks approval to exceed certain dimensional limits on its 4.07-acre Lot L.

WED, OCT 21 · 9:30 AMSan Miguel County
San Miguel County Board of County Commissioners Meeting
The headline work here is a suite of four Land Use Code amendments that would add an entirely new Article 6 — Natural Resources — to San Miguel County's code. Three sections get individual public hearings: forestry practices (Section 6-4), oil and gas operations (Section 6-5), and deep geothermal operations (also called Section 6-4 in the agenda, likely a numbering error to watch). A fourth resolution then amends Articles 1, 3, 4, and 5 to align existing code with the new natural-resources framework. That's a significant rewrite of how the county regulates extraction and land management. On the consent agenda: a 2027 Gondola IGA supplement and the Lawson Hill Connector Trail construction agreement.

Two substantive action items headline this meeting. Town of Telluride Planning & Zoning Commission
The October 22 P&Z meeting carries two items of broader public consequence. First, the Shandoka Lot Redevelopment — a Preliminary PUD application for Town-owned property at 860 Black Bear Rd — finally gets its hearing after being continued from the September 24 meeting due to staff capacity. The proposal would increase certain dimensional limitations in exchange for public benefits on the 4.07-acre Accommodations 2-zoned parcel.

A proposed 900-space parking garage at Shandoka would be one of the largest structures in the region. Town of Telluride HARC Meeting
The October 21 HARC meeting is anchored by the Shandoka Lot Redevelopment Project — the Town of Telluride's own large-scale development at 860 Black Bear Rd, zoned Accommodations 2. HARC takes up Buildings 1 and 2 as one public hearing item and Buildings 3 and 4 as a second, both continued from August 19. Each application covers new construction outside the Telluride Historic Landmark District exceeding 5,000 square feet of floor area under LUC 7-203.B.2.a.1.
