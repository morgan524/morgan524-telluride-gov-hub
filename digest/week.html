The Week Ahead Outlook
November 9 - November 15, 2026
📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

Monday, the Telluride Intergovernmental Meeting, the Telluride Open Space Commission, and the San Miguel Basin Fair Board all convene — none have posted agendas yet, so watch for those.

Tuesday brings a full slate on the north end of the county: the Ouray County Board of County Commissioners meets in regular session and again in a work session, the Ouray Planning Commission Regular Meeting is on the calendar (its agenda is typically posted to BoardBook about a week out), and the Norwood Water Commission Meeting also takes place.

Wednesday, the Ridgway Town Council Regular Meeting and the Norwood Board of Trustees Meeting are both on the schedule, with no agendas posted yet.

Thursday wraps the week with the San Miguel County Planning Commission Meeting and the SMART Board of Directors — again, no agendas available at this time.

→ Featured Organization
Telluride Mountain Club

Every cleared deadfall, via ferrata cable, and trail reroute around here has hands behind it — most of them volunteer. TMC is how the valley’s human-powered recreation actually gets built and maintained.

How you can help: Join a trail workday, become a member, donate, or report trail conditions on their online map.
