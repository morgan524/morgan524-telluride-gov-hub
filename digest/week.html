📅 The Week Ahead Outlook

The biggest thing on the plate this week is Tuesday's Town Council meeting, where a newly sworn-in Council takes up second reading of the Colorado Wildfire Resiliency Code — real changes to the Land Use Code around landscaping, trees, and historic review that align Telluride with state wildfire requirements, and worth paying attention to if you care about what gets planted or built near you. Wednesday brings a Board of County Commissioners special meeting, and Thursday Planning and Zoning digs into the Carhenge Redevelopment at 700 W Pacific, a Town-owned site that could reshape that corner of the west end. In between, there's a free reggae show Thursday at Oak Street Park, a Town Talk on cancer-fighting viruses Tuesday evening at the Conference Center, and Josh Abbott at the Sheridan Wednesday night.

The headline item is second reading of the Colorado Wildfire Resiliency Code (CWRC) — amendments to the Land Use Code covering historic/architectural review and landscaping/tree standards that bring the town in line with state wildfire resiliency requirements. THU, JUL 23Town of Telluride
Planning & Zoning Commission
Three Town-owned development projects dominate this agenda — all in the Accommodations 2 zone, all designed by Design Workshop, all on land the Town itself owns. The Carhenge Redevelopment Project gets two bites of the apple: a Preliminary Large Scale Subdivision to consolidate Lots 34 and 34B at 700 W Pacific Ave into a single parcel over 15,000 sq ft, and a Conceptual PUD for new construction on that consolidated lot (carried over from May).
