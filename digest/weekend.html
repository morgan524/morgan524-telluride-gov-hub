📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

Telluride Theatre's 36th annual Shakespeare in the Park brings Henry V to the Town Park Stage on Friday — free and pay-what-you-can, and after 36 years it's still one of the best things this valley does.

If you're in Ridgway Saturday, Ridgway Rocks brings Old Man Polly, Organtic, and PSYLO to Hartwell Park, and there's also a wildfire preparedness open house that afternoon worth stopping into — free food, local experts, and gift card giveaways.
