📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

The 41st Annual Ridgway Rendezvous Arts & Crafts Festival is the centerpiece of the weekend — two full days of free live music at Hartwell Park, Saturday from 9:30 to 5 and Sunday from 10 to 4, with artists, food vendors, and a lineup that runs from jazz and blues to roots and Americana. Closer to home, First Friday brings live music and local photography to Fabula in Ridgway Friday night, and the Telluride Farmers Market is back on South Oak Street Friday from 10:30 to 3:30 with the best of what's growing and made within 100 miles of here.
