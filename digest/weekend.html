📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

Friday has a full card if you know where to look — Oktoberfest is back in Mountain Village, so dust off the lederhosen and make an evening of it. Over in Ridgway, the same address on North Cora Street is hosting both FLANNEL FEEDBACK and WOWZERS for a double shot of live music, and if you want something with a little more structure, Crystal Creek Band plays a folk-rock-bluegrass-funk mix at Floating Lotus Brewery from six to nine for a ten-dollar cover.

Saturday, the headliner worth planning around is Alysha Brilla at The Sherbino in Ridgway — dancehall setup, limited seating, so if you want a reserved spot get on that. Closer to home, the Ouray Library Fall Book Fair runs at the Ouray Community Center benefiting the local library, and out at Frontier Field the Ridgway Star Party kicks off at seven-thirty with the Black Canyon Astronomical Society, professional telescopes, and laser-guided constellation tours — free, weather permitting, and a genuinely good reason to be outside.

Sunday, if you've got kids or just like paying attention to what the mountains are actually doing right now, the Nature in Four Seasons: Growing Dormant hike meets at the Ouray Visitor Center with SJMA for a fall naturalist walk worth your time.
