📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

Blues & Brews takes over Town Park this weekend, which means the valley is loud and alive in the best way — and if you're heading to the festival, take fifteen minutes to help assemble Emergency Go Kits for vulnerable families across San Miguel, Ouray, and Montrose Counties right there on-site. Over in Ridgway, Old Man Polly plays live Saturday, and Colona Community Church is throwing its 114th Annual Harvest Dinner — free to all, out at the Colona Stockyard.
