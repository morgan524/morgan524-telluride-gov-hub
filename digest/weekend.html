📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

The big draw this Friday is Pink Talking Fish at the Sheridan Opera House, weaving together Pink Floyd, Talking Heads, and Phish into one seamless night — a rare kind of show for a Friday in the box canyon. Down valley, Ouray is deep into its 150th birthday weekend: Saturday brings a free concert at Fellin Park with Big Head Todd and the Monsters and Hazel Miller & The Collective set against the San Juans, and Friday's Ouray 150th Gala at the Beaumont Hotel kicks it all off in style. Back in Ridgway, Saturday's free ArtAbout lets you walk into working artist studios, and Sunday the Ridgway 1K Rally Through the Alley turns a six-block downhill into a costumed, bacon-fueled party that spills straight into San Juan Oktoberfest.
