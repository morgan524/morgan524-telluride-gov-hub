📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

The Meditations — the legendary Jamaican roots reggae vocal group — are bringing their harmonies to the Sheridan Opera House Friday night, and that alone is worth clearing your calendar for. Saturday, the Telluride Theater Fringe Project puts La Familia Music Group on that same storied stage, and over in Montrose, the 2nd Annual Zumbathon raises money for Second Chance Humane Society with three hours of dancing for a $20 donation.

→ Featured Organization
Second Chance Humane Society

Since 1994 the region’s stray and surrendered pets have had somewhere to land — and the thrift shops in Ridgway and Telluride quietly help fund the whole operation.

How you can help: Adopt or foster (applications on their site), volunteer at the shelter, donate, or drop off goods at either thrift shop.
