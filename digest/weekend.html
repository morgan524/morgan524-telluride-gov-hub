📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

The big one this Saturday is the Ouray County Pride After Party at the Wright Opera House — an 18+ night of music, drag, dancing, and community in one of the most storied venues in the region. If you want to get outside after dark, Ridgway State Park has a free star party Saturday evening with the Black Canyon Astronomical Society and high-powered telescopes for all ages. And the Telluride Farmers Market is back on South Oak Street Friday, 10:30 to 3:30, with everything sourced within 100 miles.

The Pinhead Institute

A Smithsonian affiliate in a box canyon — Pinhead has been getting local kids elbow-deep in real science for years, and its free Punk Science demos are a fixture in both Telluride and Ridgway.

How you can help: Bring the kids to a free Punk Science event, enroll them in an after-school class, donate, or mentor or host a Pintern.
