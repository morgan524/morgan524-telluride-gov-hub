📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

Sunday's Evening with Ken Burns at the Palm Theatre — a screening of Episode 5 of his Vietnam series, followed by a Q&A and book signing — is the kind of event this valley does well and worth clearing your calendar for. Before you get there, Darrell Scott plays a solo show Saturday night at the Sherbino in Ridgway, and the San Juan Chamber MusicFest wraps its week with a festival concert at the Wright Opera House featuring Max Levinson and the Ulysses Quartet.
