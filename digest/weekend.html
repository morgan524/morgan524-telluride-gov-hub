📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

Friday has a few good reasons to get out. Oktoberfest is back in Mountain Village — grab your lederhosen and make a night of it. If you'd rather stay closer to a barstool, the Crystal Creek Band plays Floating Lotus Brewery from 6 to 9 PM for a ten-dollar cover, mixing folk, bluegrass, rock, and funk with a four-piece that clearly enjoys what they do. And don't sleep on the Ridgway Farmers Market at Hartwell Park — a good last chance to stock up from the people who actually grow and make things around here.

Saturday the valley spreads out nicely. Up in Ridgway, Alysha Brilla takes the stage at The Sherbino in a dancehall-style setup with some reserved seating available — presented with Pickin' Productions and worth the drive. Earlier in the day, the Nature in Four Seasons: Growing Dormant hike out of the Ouray Visitor Center is a quiet, grounded way to pay attention to what the mountains are doing as winter closes in. The Ouray Library Fall Book Fair at the Ouray Community Center is also Saturday if you need an excuse to browse.

Sunday evening, head out to Frontier Field for the Ridgway Star Party — the Black Canyon Astronomical Society sets up professional telescopes at the Ridgway Athletic Park starting at 7:30 PM, free and open to everyone, weather permitting. Laser-guided constellation tours, dark skies, and good company. Hard to beat that for a Sunday night.
