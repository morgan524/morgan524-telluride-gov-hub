📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

Sunday's Music and Makers Fest in Ouray's Fellin Park is the anchor of the weekend — four live acts running from mid-morning through late afternoon, plus an artisan market, food trucks, and a beer tent with the San Juans looking over the whole thing. Friday opens strong too, with the Farmers Market on South Oak Street and First Friday bringing Donny Morales to the Sherbino in Ridgway for a free evening of soul-acoustic-funk alongside the Art Walk through downtown.
