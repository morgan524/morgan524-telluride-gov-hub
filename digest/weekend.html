📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

Saturday's Mountains to the Desert Classic is the anchor of the weekend — the M2D's 22nd year as a major fundraiser for this community, and worth knowing about if you haven't been following it. Also on Saturday, Norwood Pioneer Days And Car Show brings that good small-town energy over in Norwood, and Ouray High School Volleyball hosts a full-day round robin at the high school with four teams going at it. Friday both the Telluride Farmers Market and the Ridgway Farmers Market are running their last weeks of the season, and Sunday night Slap Dragon closes things out with a dancehall show at The Sherbino in Ridgway — doors at seven.
