📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

The biggest thing going on this weekend is Noche de Luz at the Telluride Innovation Center Friday night — a fundraiser for Thrive's Multicultural Advocacy Programs with food from local immigrant-owned restaurants, artisan displays, and live Chicano funk from Los Mocochetes out of Denver. Saturday, Camp Vibrant takes over Ouray's Rotary Park with an all-night lineup of music and DJs starting at 4pm, and the Woman's Club of Ouray County hosts its 3rd Annual Chili Cook-Off at Fellin Park where you can taste every entry for $14.

Telluride Mountain Club

Every cleared deadfall, via ferrata cable, and trail reroute around here has hands behind it — most of them volunteer. TMC is how the valley’s human-powered recreation actually gets built and maintained.

How you can help: Join a trail workday, become a member, donate, or report trail conditions on their online map.
