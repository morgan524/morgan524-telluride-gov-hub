📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

The big draw this weekend is Sunday's Home Tour & House Show in Ridgway's Dallas Meadows neighborhood, where the Home Trust of Ouray County is pairing a walk through some beautiful local homes with a live finale from Emily Scott Robinson — an artist who's been sitting at the top of the Folk Radio charts. Before that, Saturday's Top Chef & Taste of Telluride brings local chefs head-to-head in a competition that benefits One to One Mentoring, and Friday the New York Philharmonic Principal Brass Quintet rolls into town for Telluride Chamber Music.
