📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

The 41st Annual Ridgway Rendezvous runs all weekend at Hartwell Park — free live music both days, local artists, and food vendors in one of the valley's most reliably good late-summer gatherings. Friday kicks things off with First Friday at Fabula in Ridgway, where Old Man Polly brings some serious vocal energy alongside Natalie Heller's photography, and the Telluride Farmers Market is open on South Oak Street until 3:30 if you need provisions first.

True North Youth Program

A space and a support system for every teen in the region — free, year-round, and built around helping kids find their footing on the way to adulthood. Parents say it’s where their teens build confidence and get woven into the community.

How you can help: Volunteer at a trail-work day or event (their activities calendar lists openings), donate, or — if you’re a teen — fill out a waiver (English or Spanish) and show up.
