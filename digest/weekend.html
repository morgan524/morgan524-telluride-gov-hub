📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

The biggest thing happening this Friday is Randy Houser taking the stage at the Sheridan Opera House for the first time ever — a benefit concert for the Telluride Foundation's Good Neighbor Fund, with local Colorado duo LVDY opening the night. Over in Mountain Village, Sway Wild plays Reflection Plaza at 5 PM as part of the Music on the Green series, and come Sunday, Heather & Douglas bring their folk-roots harmonies to the Courtyard at 610 in Ridgway for an intimate outdoor evening.
