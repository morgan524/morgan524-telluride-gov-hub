📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

The Meditations — the legendary Jamaican roots reggae vocal group — bring their harmonies to the Sheridan Opera House Friday night, and that alone is worth clearing your calendar for. Saturday, Down Valley Park marks its 20th anniversary with free food and live music from Telluride Gold Kings from 4 to 6 p.m., and the Sheridan hosts La Familia Music Group's free bilingual community concert, capping a day of youth DJ and beat-making workshops open to kids 10 to 18.

Second Chance Humane Society

Since 1994 the region’s stray and surrendered pets have had somewhere to land — and the thrift shops in Ridgway and Telluride quietly help fund the whole operation.

How you can help: Adopt or foster (applications on their site), volunteer at the shelter, donate, or drop off goods at either thrift shop.
