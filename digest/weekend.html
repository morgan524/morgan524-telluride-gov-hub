📅 The Weekend Ahead Outlook

Blues & Brews takes over Town Park this weekend, and if you're heading down for the music, carve out fifteen minutes to help assemble Emergency Go Kits at the festival — free preparedness supplies headed to vulnerable families across San Miguel, Ouray, and Montrose Counties, which feels like the right thing to do in the middle of a good time. Over in Norwood, the Wild Roots Revival gathers at the Livery on Saturday for a full day of community and inner exploration tied to the Fall Equinox, and up in Ouray, the Mountain Trail Run loops the whole perimeter of town to raise money for the school.
