Hub-Bub civic forum

Find the local conversation without digging through the noise.

Hub-Bub is organized around the way people actually look for civic information: by topic, meeting, source, question, and next step. Use it to ask a precise question, share a public document, summarize a meeting, or propose a practical solution.

Start a discussion Browse current posts Guidelines

Designed for useful participation

  1. Pick a topic. Housing, land use, budget, water, schools, events, or governance.
  2. Choose a post type. Question, source, meeting takeaway, solution, or community need.
  3. Add a next step. Read, attend, email, volunteer, ask, or watch.
Best use: help neighbors understand what is happening early enough to participate.

Forum guidelines

Ask clear questions — good questions lead to better answers.
Share sources — attach documents, link to meeting minutes.
Attack ideas, not people — disagree vigorously but constructively.
Propose solutions — practical ideas move the conversation forward.
