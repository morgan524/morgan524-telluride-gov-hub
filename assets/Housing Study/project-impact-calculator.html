    Method & sources.
    • Staffing 1.5–2.5/key and the jobs→workers→households factors are stated-range extrapolations [E]; commercial rate is the adopted Telluride LUC rate. Replace with each project's mitigation filings when public.
    • Delivery costs are the region's demonstrated results: Sunnyside ($516k TDC, free county land), VooDoo ($1.26M TDC), Meadowlark (~$137k net town subsidy). See the housing baseline and the public report, Sections 4–5.
    • Wastewater: 70–100 gal/person/day; guest pillows ≈ 2.2/key; food & beverage, spa, and laundry add high-strength load (shown as the mid-range person-equivalent bump). Headroom denominator: ~1.05 MGD, the new 2.67 MGD Ilium plant minus ~1.6 MGD current peak (report endnote 62).
    • Traffic: ~5 trips/key/day resort generation plus one round-trip per commuting worker; comparator from the Diamond Ridge study — a 240-unit project advances Society Turn roundabout failure to ~2027.
    • Estimates only, for public discussion. Livable Telluride welcomes corrections.
