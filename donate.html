Where Your Donation Goes

100% of contributions go to keeping the site running and the community informed.

Independent Reporting

Funds the time it takes to attend public meetings, read agendas, and translate dense government documents into plain-language summaries you can act on.

Amplifying Local Voices

Keeps Hub-Bub, the comment threads, and the local-orgs directory free and open to every resident, no paywall and no ads.

Site Infrastructure

Covers hosting, domain, email delivery, and the data pipelines that pull from county/town/school-district sources so meeting info stays current.
