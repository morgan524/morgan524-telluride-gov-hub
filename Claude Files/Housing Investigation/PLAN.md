## The six questions

1. **Carrying capacity of the East End** — buildable-land inventory (FLU ×
   slopes × easements × existing development) + infrastructure envelopes
   (wastewater, water, Hwy 145/transit) + peer frameworks (TRPA, Aspen GMQS,
   Jackson/Teton, Whistler bed-unit cap).
2. **What housing doesn't sit vacant** — revealed preference: waitlists vs.
   vacancies by AMI tier × unit size (VCA capped waitlist vs. ~49 vacant
   town-managed units; 150–175% AMI product struggles).
3. **Full cost per subsidized unit by AMI** — development cost minus
   rent-supportable debt + operating shortfall + forgone taxes + land +
   reserves + admin. Real costs from cost tracker / VooDoo work.
4. **Secondary costs of supply** — schools, transit, utilities expansion,
   housing-dept staffing, portfolio capex liability, tax-base removal.
5. **Induced growth** — need-catching vs. growth-accommodating line; the
   jobs-housing spiral; peer-town evidence; simple explicit model.
6. **Missing questions** — buy-down vs. build; region as unit of analysis;
   year-30 portfolio liability; measurable success metrics; the Jan-2025
   income-based-rent natural experiment.
