Project Finance Summary

Total Cost of Project—Total loan payments over term + land cost + upfront payments.
Total Cost of Housing—Housing construction only — residential sf × cost / sf input. Excludes land, garage, and shared facilities.
Cost Per Unit—Total cost divided by total housing units.
Cost per Square Foot—Total cost divided by total residential + commercial square footage.
Average Rental Price based on AMI levels—Monthly rent per affordable unit at the current AMI mix.
Required Sale Price of Market Units—Per market unit, to fully cover project cost net of rent-supportable debt.
Required per sf Price of Market Units—Per square foot for the market units to break even.
Total Possible Income by AMI Level—Modeled residential rent capacity at the project's AMI mix + commercial rent estimate.
Annual Debt Service (Fully Amortized)—No balloon; full construction principal amortized over term.
Balloon Payment—No balloon under fully-amortized structure.
Market-Rate Sales Estimate—Unverified estimate — to be updated when specific sale prices are disclosed.
